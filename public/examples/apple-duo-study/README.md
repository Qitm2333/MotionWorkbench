# Apple Duo interaction study

This is the runnable, local-resource snapshot used by MotionWorkbench. It contains the readable study adapter, the required Apple/Lotus runtime modules, and the assets named by `asset-manifest.json`.

The larger upstream source archive and research tooling remain in the original study directory and are intentionally not duplicated here. `EVIDENCE.md` records provenance and the source-to-behavior analysis. The sample is for study and reproduction testing, not redistribution as a product asset library.
